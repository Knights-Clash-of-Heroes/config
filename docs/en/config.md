### 2. Campaign and events

The campaign is the story mode of the game, where the player must complete quests to advance the story. Events are unlocked after a specific level, recur frequently, or happen during a specific period. They are node-based, and each node has one or more quests. A quest can be a battle, a requirement to collect an item, or a charge of a resource to complete it.
